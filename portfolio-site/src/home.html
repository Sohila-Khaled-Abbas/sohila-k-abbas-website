I’m Sohila, a Data Analyst and BI Developer based in Egypt. My path into data is a career shift built through hands-on projects, structured study, and a lot of asking “why?”

I enjoy the whole journey: untangling a dataset, designing its model, building the report, and connecting the repetitive pieces with automation. I’m especially interested in analytics that reflects the Egyptian market.

Along the way, I turn what I learn into guides, labs, and resources other people can use. That’s the idea behind The Data Tea.
